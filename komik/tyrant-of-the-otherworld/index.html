Tyrant of the Otherworld: My Shadow Can Evolve Infinitely

Manhua 7.5 111 Chapter
Action Fantasy

In the year 2035, rifts to alien realms tore through the world’s defenses. Swarms of insectoids and beastkin ravaged the land, and humanity struggled to survive through the strength of “Evolvers.” Li Yi was betrayed by his teammates during an attack by alien beings and left for dead. On the brink of death, he awakened the “Titan Shadow” System—his shadow could evolve without limits! It could devour ores to enhance defense, tear through enemies to form deadly blades, and even split into an army of shadow soldiers… From insect hives to haunted lands, wherever his shadow passed, enemies were shredded into pulp. This is the revenge of the weak—begun with a shadow that devours all.
